About

Everything Begins with a Draught

At The Draughtsman London Ltd, we believe every project begins with a draught. A draught is more than just a drawing, it's the first act of creation, the line that turns vision into reality.

From measured surveys and planning applications to building regulations, party wall notices, and advanced LiDAR and drone inspections, our work is built on clarity, accuracy, and trust. Each project starts with a precise draught that sets the foundation for smooth approvals and confident construction.

Our story began the same way, small drawings for homeowners that grew into a practice combining traditional surveying expertise with modern technology. Today, we continue to transform those early lines into approvals, buildings, and lasting results.

Because whether on paper, in writing, or in bricks and mortar everything begins with a draught.

  • Clear, collaborative briefing so the output matches your ambition
  • Digital capture and traditional detailing for reliable documentation
  • Flexible site visits and swift turnaround across London and surrounding areas
Services

Surveying support that moves projects forward

Choose a single service or combine packages for a seamless end-to-end experience.

Measured Surveys

Detailed building surveys for refurbishments, extensions and valuations, delivered with the precision needed to unlock accurate design work.

  • Laser-accurate floor plans, elevations and sections
  • Planning-ready drawing packs for local authorities
  • Ideal for estimators, project managers and design teams

Planning Applications

From feasibility to submission, we prepare and coordinate documentation that meets planning policy while communicating the story of your proposal.

  • Feasibility reviews to flag constraints early
  • Design development aligned with planning strategy
  • Complete application packs and submission support

Drone Inspection

CAA-licensed drone surveys capture 4K imagery for roofs, façades and hard-to-reach details, giving you safe and rapid insights.

  • High-resolution imagery and video deliverables
  • Pinpoint maintenance issues before they escalate
  • A2 CofC certified pilots with full insurance cover

Party Wall Matters

Guidance and documentation aligned with the Party Wall Act 1996 to manage neighbour agreements and keep construction programmes on track.

  • Party Wall Act 1996 notices drafted and served
  • Schedules of condition with supporting photography
  • Coordination with adjoining owners and surveyors
